# UK Business Travel Standard 2025

## Meals

<!-- rule: {"key": "travel.meal_limit", "value": "45", "unit": "GBP/day", "label": "UK meal allowance", "statement": "The UK business-travel meal cap is GBP 45 per day.", "aliases": ["meal allowance", "food expenses", "UK travel"]} -->

Employees travelling inside the United Kingdom may claim actual meal costs up to the daily cap. Alcohol is excluded.

## London accommodation

<!-- rule: {"key": "travel.hotel_london", "value": "180", "unit": "GBP/night", "label": "London hotel cap", "statement": "The London accommodation cap is GBP 180 per night.", "aliases": ["London hotel", "accommodation"]} -->

The cap applies to the room rate including mandatory taxes. Conference hotels need prior approval when above the cap.
